# Inspiration for My Game Project

My inspiration for this game project comes from the *Papa’s Pancakeria* game I used to play on CoolMathForkids.com growing up. I found it fascinating how a repetitive task like flipping pancakes could become engaging through timing and visual cues. There is something satisfying about watching the pancakes change color, waiting for the perfect moment, and landing a good flip.  

Instead of replicating the full functionality of *Papa’s Pancakeria*—taking orders, cooking, plating, etc.—I wanted to scale it down into a single-station minigame. In my version, the player flips packages that are timed, and points are awarded based on the precision of each flip. This keeps the gameplay simple, focused, and fun while still capturing the satisfying mechanics of the original game.
